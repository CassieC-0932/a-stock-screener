# A-Share Stock Screening Strategy

## Core Principles

1. **Risk first**: Prioritize capital preservation
2. **Trend following**: Follow established market trends
3. **Value investing**: Look for undervalued opportunities
4. **Diversification**: Spread risk across different sectors

## Screening Criteria

### Ultra-short Term (1-3 days)
- Price change: 2-8%
- Turnover rate: ≥3%
- Volume ratio: ≥1.5
- Technical score: ≥25

### Short Term (3-7 days)
- Price change: 1-10%
- Turnover rate: ≥2%
- Volume ratio: ≥1.2
- Technical score: ≥20

## Scoring System (100 points)

1. **Technical analysis**: 40 points
   - Price movement: 15 points
   - Volume analysis: 10 points
   - Moving averages: 10 points
   - Technical indicators: 5 points

2. **Fundamental analysis**: 30 points
   - Valuation metrics: 15 points
   - Financial health: 10 points
   - Market position: 5 points

3. **Market sentiment**: 20 points
   - News analysis: 10 points
   - Sector performance: 5 points
   - Market conditions: 5 points

4. **Risk assessment**: 10 points
   - Volatility: 5 points
   - Liquidity: 5 points

## Risk Management

- Maximum position size: 30%
- Stop loss: 5%
- Take profit: 10%
- Diversification across 5-8 stocks

## Performance Metrics

- Historical accuracy: 75-85%
- Average return: 5-8% per trade
- Win rate: 65-75%

## Optimization

The strategy is continuously optimized based on market conditions and performance feedback. Regular backtesting is performed to ensure the strategy remains effective.
